Where the numbers come from. Elevation, distance and the climb figures are from the official LiveTrail course file (CMTUTMB2026_HMONG50D), as analysed in the Aug 29 GPX teardown: the 2 m filter reproduces the published 2,951 m at every aid station. The profile and 3D heights use that file resampled to 100 m and smoothed over 70 m, so very short steep pieces read gentler here than in the climb cards (the wall shows ~18% on the model, 20.3% in the raw file).

Where the line on the ground comes from. The horizontal track is your Strava route copy of the course (1,992 points, 61.0 km), stretched evenly to the official 62.88 km. Checked against the teardown: it passes 662 m from Wat Phra That Doi Suthep at km 49.0, against 674 m at km 49.1 in the official file, and its start and finish sit 173 m apart (169 m official).

What is not shown. There is no terrain surface. The ground is a flat reference plane at 250 m and the shaded curtain drops from the track to it. Clock times assume the 12:30 finish plan from the teardown (06:00 start, no aid stops). The course can change: re-download the GPX in November and check km 49–52 (the Sai Yoi → Montha Than trail closure) in person.
